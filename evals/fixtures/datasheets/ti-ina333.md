SYNTHETIC SUMMARY FOR SOFTWARE TESTS - NOT MANUFACTURER DOCUMENTATION.

## Features
Rail-to-rail output instrumentation amplifier, gain set with one external resistor:
G = 1 + 100 kΩ / RG. Supply 1.8 V to 5.5 V, low quiescent current, MSOP-8 (VSSOP-8) package.
Typical uses: EEG and ECG front ends, bridge amplifiers, medical instrumentation.
