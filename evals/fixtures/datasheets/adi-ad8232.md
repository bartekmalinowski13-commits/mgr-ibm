# Analog Devices AD8232 single-lead heart rate monitor front end (synthetic test datasheet)
SYNTHETIC SUMMARY FOR SOFTWARE TESTS - NOT MANUFACTURER DOCUMENTATION.

## Features
Integrated signal conditioning block for ECG and other biopotential measurements.
Instrumentation amplifier, operational amplifier, right leg drive amplifier, lead-off detection,
fast restore. Single supply 2.0 V to 3.5 V, low supply current, 20-lead 4 mm x 4 mm LFCSP.

## Applications
Fitness and activity heart rate monitors, portable ECG, remote health monitors.
