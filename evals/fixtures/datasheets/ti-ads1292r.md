SYNTHETIC SUMMARY FOR SOFTWARE TESTS - NOT MANUFACTURER DOCUMENTATION.

## Features
Two low-noise programmable gain amplifiers and two 24-bit delta-sigma ADCs, built-in respiration
impedance measurement, right leg drive, lead-off detection, internal oscillator and reference.
TQFP-32 package. Interface SPI.
