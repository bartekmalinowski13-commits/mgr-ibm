# Yageo RC0603 thick film chip resistors (synthetic test datasheet)
SYNTHETIC SUMMARY FOR SOFTWARE TESTS - NOT MANUFACTURER DOCUMENTATION.

## Features
General purpose thick film chip resistor, size 0603 (1608 metric). Tolerance 1 % (F) or 5 % (J).
Power rating 0.1 W at 70 °C. Resistance range 1 Ω to 10 MΩ, zero ohm jumper available.
