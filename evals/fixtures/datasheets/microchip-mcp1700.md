# Microchip MCP1700 low quiescent current LDO regulator (synthetic test datasheet)
SYNTHETIC SUMMARY FOR SOFTWARE TESTS - NOT MANUFACTURER DOCUMENTATION.

## Features
250 mA low dropout linear regulator with about 1.6 µA quiescent current, input up to 6 V.
Fixed output voltages including 1.8 V, 2.5 V, 3.0 V and 3.3 V. Packages SOT-23, SOT-89, TO-92.
Stable with 1 µF ceramic capacitors. Good for battery powered wearables.

## Ordering information
MCP1700T-3302E/TT: 3.3 V output, SOT-23, tape and reel.
MCP1700T-1802E/TT: 1.8 V output, SOT-23, tape and reel.
